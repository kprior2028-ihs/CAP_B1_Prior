My Best Work

I chose my app Rogue Locks as my best work because it is the longest and prettiest of my work. I really love the color palette on this app and the layout. It stands out to me because it feels like an app I would use. I found it a little difficult to code every last thing I wanted to be interactive. I think it was easy and fun to design it and make it super cute and easy to understand. I used all my knowledge of creating screens and designing them, also the changing between screens, adding images, and how to create my own sprites/icons inside my app.
