This game showcases my growth because this was the first true game I coded. It was really challenging to do the movement and scoring, and it's definitely far from perfect. It was hard to figure out how the scoring worked, and there were definitely moments I wanted to give up.I remember this big bug where all my sprites would fall through or just stop mid-screen, and that was really frustrating. I worked together with some of my classmates,and we helped each other find the bug, but in the end, my game came out pretty good! I put hours into this game, and I feel like if I hadn't put in half the effort I did, I wouldn't put it on this page. I would probably change the scoring now that I know a little more about it and how to count it correctly.
